# Discussion (paper-ready draft)

The corrected experiment tests whether UAV-enabled dispatch retains a model-internal benefit after explicit fixed-site, return, weather, duty, and battery constraints. The paired comparisons therefore estimate the effect of enabling the UAV option within this dispatcher and parameterization. They do not estimate a real-world causal effect of drone deployment.

The results should be interpreted against four evidence limits. First, demand is synthetic even though orders are frozen and identical across modes. Second, the FCD input is a direction-unobserved speed proxy. Third, gridded weather was not validated against an independent local station, and the 10 km screen is an audit threshold rather than a safety boundary. Fourth, station permission, WMP228 operational clearance, and Wingcopter field energy remain unresolved. These limits prevent claims of verified operational feasibility or life-cycle decarbonization.

The main contribution of the corrected analysis is methodological: road, weather, service, and physical resource ledgers are evaluated with a common control and a seed-level uncertainty unit. Any advantage that survives these constraints is conditional on the tested system. Any loss of the historical advantage is also scientifically informative because it identifies sensitivity to operational assumptions.
