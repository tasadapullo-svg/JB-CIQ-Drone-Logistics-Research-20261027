# Conclusions and limitations (paper-ready draft)

Under the corrected simulation, M0, M1, and M2 can be compared on identical frozen tasks, shared vehicle resources, all-order service denominators, and physical energy ledgers. The results support conditional statements about the tested configurations only. They do not establish actual aircraft permission, field energy consumption, lifecycle emissions, observed demand response, or universal superiority of UAV delivery.

Publication claims should therefore use the terms conditional simulation, operational emissions proxy, and model-internal paired comparison. Claims that UAV delivery is proven to reduce lifecycle emissions, is legally flyable at the studied sites, or is economically superior in practice are not supported by the available evidence.
